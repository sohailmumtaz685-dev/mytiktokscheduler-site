Storyforge Social

Schedule your own TikTok videos — calmly, in batches, on your own computer.

Storyforge Social is a personal scheduling tool for TikTok creators. Connect your own TikTok account with TikTok's official OAuth login, add your videos to a queue with a caption and a time, and the tool publishes them for you at the scheduled time using TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API). This website is the product's public home: what the tool does, how it works, and the legal pages below. There is no sign-up or login on this website — the tool itself runs on the creator's own machine.

Features

Official TikTok login only. You approve access on TikTok's own site. Storyforge Social never sees or stores your TikTok password, and you can disconnect or revoke access at any time in TikTok Settings.
A simple publishing queue. Add a video, write its caption, pick a date and time. The queue shows what is scheduled, what has posted, and what needs attention.
Direct Post via the official API. At the scheduled time the video is published to the connected account through TikTok's Content Posting API — the same approval and audience rules as posting in the TikTok app apply.
Your data stays yours. The tool runs locally for a single creator. No accounts are sold, no data is shared with advertisers, and the Privacy Policy below states exactly what is processed and why.

How it works

  1. Open the tool and choose Connect TikTok. You approve access on TikTok's own site — we never see your TikTok password.
  2. Add a video, write its caption, and pick a date and time.
  3. At that time, the video is published to the connected account. You can disconnect or revoke access at any time in TikTok Settings.

Who it is for

Creators who manage their own TikTok accounts and want to prepare posts in batches instead of uploading one by one — for example, recording a week of videos in one sitting and letting the schedule do the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Is Storyforge Social affiliated with TikTok?
No. It is an independent tool that uses TikTok's official developer APIs, and it is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

Do you store my TikTok password?
No. Login happens entirely on TikTok's own site through OAuth. The tool stores only the access tokens TikTok issues, on your own machine, so it can post at the times you scheduled.

Can I stop a scheduled post?
Yes. Remove it from the queue before its time, or disconnect the account — either stops future posts.

What does it cost?
Storyforge Social is a personal tool provided as-is for the creator's own accounts; see the Terms of Service.
