Social Media

Social Media is a personal scheduling tool for TikTok creators. Connect your own TikTok account with TikTok's official OAuth login, add your videos to a queue with a caption and a time, and the tool publishes them for you at the scheduled time using TikTok's official APIs.

How it works

  1. Sign in and choose Connect TikTok. You approve access on TikTok's own site — we never see your TikTok password.
  2. Add a video, write its caption, and pick a date and time.
  3. At that time, the video is published to the connected account. You can disconnect or revoke access at any time in TikTok Settings.

Who it is for

Creators who manage their own TikTok accounts and want to prepare posts in batches instead of uploading one by one.
