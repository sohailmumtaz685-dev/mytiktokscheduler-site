Privacy Policy — Social Media

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Operator: the Social Media operator. Contact: the email address shown on the app listing in the TikTok developer portal.

Social Media is a scheduling tool that lets a TikTok account owner schedule and publish their own videos. This policy explains what data the service handles.

Data we access

  • When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok provides us an access token, a refresh token, and your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, under the scopes you approve (user.info.basic and any posting scopes shown on the TikTok approval screen).
  • Videos, captions and scheduled times that you create in the service.

How we use it

  • Tokens are used only to publish content you scheduled, to refresh access before publishing, and to show your connected accounts in the dashboard.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not post anything you did not schedule.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored encrypted on the service's server and are deleted when you disconnect the account or delete your data on request. Scheduled media is deleted after publication or on request.

Your choices

You can revoke this app's access at any time in TikTok Settings, which immediately stops future publishing. Contact us at the email above to request deletion of stored data.

Third parties

Data is transmitted to TikTok's official APIs to carry out publishing. TikTok's own privacy policy governs TikTok's handling of that data.
